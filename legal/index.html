Privacy Policy

This policy covers Gem Incremental, an independently developed browser game, including guest play where available. It explains what we collect, why we use it and how to contact us.

1. Who we are & how to contact us

Gem Incremental is run by its developer, Lanky. For privacy questions, account access, correction or deletion requests, use the contact form and select “Account or save problem” or “Other”. Include a reply contact and enough information to identify your account. If you cannot access the form, text 80805828, the contact number also listed there. Never send your password, sign-in codes or full payment-card details.

The Support page is for optional donations; privacy and account requests should use the contact options above.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data. An account identifier and authentication records. For permanent accounts, this includes your email address or basic profile information supplied by Google if you choose Google sign-in, plus your chosen username. Guest accounts, when enabled, also have identifiers and saved progress; “guest” does not mean no data is collected.
  • Gameplay & activity. Progress, rolls, inventory, currencies, equipment, achievements, trades and market transactions, guild activity, event participation, referral relationships and rewards, and activity timestamps used to show player presence and usage.
  • Social content & requests. Chat messages, profile and showcase details, guild names, descriptions and logos, reports and ban appeals. Contact-form submissions include the category, message, optional reply contact, account identifier when signed in and referring page.
  • Security data. IP addresses, request information, authentication activity, anti-abuse signals, moderation decisions and related logs used to investigate cheating, shared-account or referral abuse and service problems.
  • Purchases. Facets balances, claim codes, cosmetic ownership and transaction histories. When you buy Facets, we receive purchase or refund notifications from Buy Me a Coffee, including transaction identifiers, amounts, products and the information it includes in those notifications, which may include purchaser contact details. The game does not collect your full card number or card security code.
  • Browser storage & analytics. Sign-in session data, settings, referral codes, cached game information and notices saved in your browser, plus the website usage and performance measurements described below.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate you, save progress and run gameplay, social and referral features.
  • To deliver cosmetic purchases, reconcile payments and handle refunds.
  • To investigate reports and appeals, prevent abuse, enforce fair-play rules and secure the service.
  • To understand usage, diagnose performance problems and improve the game.
  • To respond to account and privacy requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

4. Who can see information & service providers

Usernames, public profiles, showcased gems, leaderboard entries and public game activity can be seen by other players or visitors. Global chat is shared with players; guild chat and membership information are shared within the relevant game features. Avoid posting sensitive personal information. Authorized administrators and moderators can access information needed to operate the game and investigate abuse.

We use these providers, subject to their respective privacy practices:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, storage and server functions for account and game data.
  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery, including technical request information.
  • Cloudflare — website analytics and performance measurements.
  • Google — optional Google sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Buy Me a Coffee — optional donations and Facets checkout, with its payment providers.

These services may process information in countries outside your own. We may also disclose information where legally required or necessary to investigate abuse and protect the service or others.

5. Guest saves & browser storage

Where guest play is enabled, progress is saved on our servers and access depends on the guest sign-in stored in your browser. It can persist across visits, but clearing browser data, signing out, using private browsing or losing that browser profile can leave you unable to recover it. Link an email or Google account through the Account page where available to protect access to your progress.

We use local storage and session storage for authentication, preferences, cached gameplay data, pending referral codes and dismissed notices. Some preferences also synchronize with your account. Clearing browser storage does not delete server-side account data. Blocking it can disrupt sign-in, saves and settings.

6. Website analytics

Pages, including this one, load Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits and page performance. Measurements can include page and referrer information, browser and device information and loading timings. Cloudflare describes this service as cookie-free and does not track individual users across websites through it. See Cloudflare’s analytics documentation. We do not use advertising cookies.

7. Retention

We retain account and game information while needed to provide the game. Reports, moderation and security records, purchase and refund records, and backups may remain for longer where needed to resolve disputes, prevent abuse or meet legal obligations. Retention depends on the purpose and the provider; we do not promise immediate erasure from every backup. Information should be deleted or de-identified when no longer needed for those purposes.

8. Your choices & requests

Use the contact options above to request access, correction or deletion, or to ask about withdrawing consent to a particular use of your data. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights, including to object, restrict processing, receive a portable copy or complain to your local data protection authority. We may need to verify account ownership before acting, and will explain any applicable limits. Removing data essential to the game can mean closing your account and losing progress and items.

You can stop playing, sign out, change your profile or settings, or clear browser storage at any time. These actions alone do not request account deletion.

9. Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 or older. Do not use it if you are under 13 or below a higher minimum age required by the law where you live. If your local law requires parental permission, obtain it before using the game. If you believe a child has provided personal information in breach of these requirements, contact us so we can investigate and arrange appropriate removal.

10. Security

We use authentication, access controls and server-side validation to protect data, but no online service is completely secure. Use a strong, unique password and keep sign-in credentials private.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before a new use of personal information. This policy describes our practices; it does not waive your data protection rights.
