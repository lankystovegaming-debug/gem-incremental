Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Gem Incremental (“the game”, “we”) collects when you play, why, and the choices you have. Gem Incremental is an independently developed browser game.

1. Who we are

Gem Incremental is a hobby project run by its developer. The best way to reach us is through the in-game Support and Bug Report pages.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data. When you first play you receive an anonymous guest account tied to your browser session. If you upgrade to a permanent account we store your email address (for email sign-in) or the basic profile from your Google account (for Google sign-in), plus a username you choose.
  • Gameplay data. Your progress and everything needed to run the game — rolls, gems, inventory, currencies, crafting, equipment, achievements, guild membership, leaderboard stats and similar.
  • Chat & social content. Messages you send in global or guild chat, guild names, tags and descriptions, and profile details you set.
  • Technical data. Your IP address and basic request information, used for security, anti-cheat and moderation.
  • Local storage. Settings such as your theme and preferences are kept in your browser’s local storage and stay on your device.

3. How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress.
  • To operate social features (chat, guilds, leaderboards).
  • To keep the game fair — detecting cheating, alt-account abuse and exploits, and enforcing the Rules.
  • To fix bugs and improve the game.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where your data lives

We rely on a few reputable providers that process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy practices:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and server functions; your account and game data are stored here.
  • Vercel — hosts and serves the website.
  • Google — optional sign-in, only if you choose it.
  • Buy Me a Coffee — if you donate, payment is handled entirely by them under their own terms; we never see your payment details.

5. Guest saves

A guest (anonymous) save lives only in the browser session that created it. If you clear your browser data or lose that session without linking an account, that save can be lost and we generally cannot recover it. Link an email or Google account to protect your progress.

6. Cookies & local storage

We use only the browser storage that is essential to make the game work — keeping you signed in and remembering your settings. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Retention

We keep account and game data while your account exists and for as long as needed to run the game and enforce the Rules. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data.

8. Your choices and rights

You can request access to, or deletion of, your account and data through the in-game Support page. Some limited information may be retained where needed for security or to prevent abuse. You can sign out or stop playing at any time.

9. Children

Gem Incremental is not intended for children under 13. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, please do not create a permanent account. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Security

We use reputable providers and server-side validation to protect data, but no online service can be perfectly secure. Please use a strong, unique password if you sign up with email.

11. Changes

Gem Incremental is a living project and this policy may change. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “last updated” date. Continuing to play means you accept the current policy.
