Field guide

How the Crystal Caverns work

The Caverns run alongside normal rolling like any expedition, but they split risk into two dials. Danger decides whether an incident happens; Instability — permanent and uncapped — decides how severe it is and how good your artifacts get. You choose how hard to dig.

Earning Extraction Progress

Each genuine gem roll adds base progress, exactly like the Abandoned Mine:

  • +1 every roll
  • +1 rarity 1 in 50 or rarer
  • +3 rarity 1 in 1,000 or rarer
  • +7 rarity 1 in 10,000 or rarer
  • +3 any mutated gem
  • +3 displayed weight at least 2× its base

Extraction Intensity

Switch intensity freely between rolls. Faster digging clears depths sooner but permanently adds Instability (measured per 50 base progress).

What Instability does

  • Better artifacts. Your artifact-find chance is multiplied by up to ×3 — it climbs from ×1 and approaches ×3 as Instability rises (≈×2.6 around 200). The final chance is still capped at 100%.
  • Worse incidents. Instability reshapes severity if an incident occurs: low Instability skews Minor, high Instability pushes toward Critical. Danger alone never makes an incident worse — it only sets how often one happens.
  • It never goes down. Instability is permanent for the run and has no ceiling. Danger, by contrast, can be reduced (see Outposts).

Decisions you will face

Crystal events

Small finds — a pocket, a resonance, a deposit. Leave them for nothing, or disturb them for a reward at the cost of Instability: +3 (Break Open), +5 (Excavate) or +8 (Trigger Resonance).

Crystal formations

Leave, pay for a Careful extraction (a higher-quality artifact search), or Shatter it. Instability from the formation applies before the artifact roll.

Stabilisation Outposts · D3 / D6 / D9

Secure banks all cargo and artifacts; Reinforce does that and drops Danger by 15. Both leave Instability untouched.

D3 $250K / $500K · D6 $1M / $1.75M · D9 $3.5M / $5.5M

The Crystal Heart · D10

Extract to end safely, or Fracture the Heart for the best rewards — this adds +30 Instability after the independent D10 artifact checks, then opens Overdepth.

Cargo, extraction & Overdepth

  • Unsecured artifacts show “AT RISK”. A new Museum discovery is only registered once you secure it (an Outpost) or extract safely — a forced extraction loses everything unsecured.
  • Extract safely whenever you are at a stopping point to keep the whole haul and register discoveries.
  • Overdepth descents are free but abandon your safe exit, pushing Danger and rewards higher. Settle the run to add its value to your account.
