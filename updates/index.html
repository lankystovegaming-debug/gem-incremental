New features, improvements, balance changes, and bug fixes.

Latest v0.8.2 18 August 2026

Late-Game Equipment

New Equipment

  • Added the T11 Eclipse, T12 Singularity, and T13 Transcendent Pickaxes.
  • Added the T9 Event Horizon and T10 Singularity Lanterns.
  • Added the T9 Eventide Boots and T10 Singularity Striders.
  • Added new late-game crafting challenges, material costs, and lifetime-roll requirements.

Pickaxe Passives

  • Mutation Resonance: Eclipse Pickaxe provides 1.10x mutation activation chances.
  • Event Horizon: Singularity Pickaxe provides 1.10x Luck toward gems with base rarity of 1/100,000 or greater.
  • Enchant Conduit: Transcendent Pickaxe increases the strength of its enchant by 10%.
  • Equipment passives work only while their specific pickaxe is equipped and never affect Relic drop rates.

Balance

  • Late-game Luck progression has been kept deliberately gradual, reaching 22x total Luck at T13.
  • Lantern progression now reaches 3.4x total Roll Speed at T10.
  • Boot progression now reaches 6.75x total Weight Luck at T10.
Latest v0.8.1 18 August 2026

Equipment Selection

Quality of Life

  • Players can now choose which unlocked equipment tier they want to equip.
  • Crafting an upgrade no longer permanently removes its prerequisite equipment.
  • Previously unlocked lower tiers have been restored for existing players.
  • Equipping an item automatically stores the other equipped item in the same category.
  • Equipment lists now show the highest unlocked tiers first.
v0.8 18 August 2026

✨ Enchants

Enchants

  • Enchants are boosts that modify your pickaxe in different ways.
  • Each pickaxe can have only one enchant at a time.
  • Eight new enchants have been added.
  • Enchanting a pickaxe again replaces its current enchant with a guaranteed different one.

Enchant Relics

  • Introduced a new special rarity: Relic.
  • The Relic collection begins with the Enchant Relic and Ancient Relic.
  • Both relics provide unique ways to empower your pickaxe.
  • Ancient Relics exhibit a stronger force, granting enhanced and Ancient-exclusive enchants.
  • Relic drop chances are completely unaffected by Luck.

v0.7.1 17 August 2026

Gems Found Leaderboard

New

  • Added a Gems Found section to the Leaderboards page.
  • Gems Found is calculated by adding together the base rarity denominators of every unmutated gem rolled.
  • For example, finding gems with rarities of 1 in 2, 1 in 8, and 1 in 100 awards a total score of 110.
  • Mutated gems are excluded from the score.
  • Gems Found scores begin tracking with the release of v0.7.1.

Fixes

  • Fixed an exploit that allowed one account to roll simultaneously across multiple browser tabs.
  • Roll cooldowns are now claimed atomically by the server, so only one concurrent roll can succeed.

Balancing

  • Reworked Weight Luck with diminishing returns, preventing high bonuses from guaranteeing a gem above 1.1× weight.
  • The high-weight region now begins at a 25% chance and gradually approaches a maximum of 85%.
  • Reduced the continuation chance for each additional multiplier in the 2×+ weight tail from 1 in 2 to 1 in 3.
v0.7.0 17 August 2026

Transcendent Expansion

Gem Incremental’s gem pool has expanded with 16 new discoveries, including real-world minerals, extraterrestrial materials, and an entirely new rarity tier.

New Gems

16 new gems:

  • 5 Mythic
  • 5 Exotic
  • 5 Cosmic
  • 1 Transcendent

A new rarity tier has been introduced for gems with rarities between 1 in 10,000,000 and 1 in 99,999,999.

Gem Index

  • Added all newly available gems to the Gem Index.
  • The new rarest gem will remain hidden in the Gem Index until discovered.
  • Once discovered, secret gems and their mutation combinations become visible in the index.
  • Updated discovery totals to account for newly revealed secret gems dynamically.
  • Added descriptions, base values, weights, and exact chances for every new gem.

Visuals

  • Added unique colors, gradients, fonts, and glow effects for every new gem.
  • Added a distinctive iridescent appearance for Transcendent gems.
  • Added Transcendent rarity badges and inventory filtering.

Balancing

  • Ja-ore has a base weight of 90kg and a value of $20 per gram, giving it a base value of $1,800,000.
  • Preserved Lanky Gem’s real-world-inspired 40.5kg base weight and approximate $4,500,000 base value.
  • Synchronized gem prices across the browser, Roll server, and Admin tools.
  • Corrected outdated server prices that did not match the previously approved economy rebalance.

Fixes

  • Restored Admin Event luck bonuses and multipliers.
  • Restored the minimum rarity floor provided by Luck.
  • Rolls now record the complete effective Luck value, including equipment, potions, and Admin Events.
  • Preserved stacked mutations, multi-tab protection, and existing Roll behavior.
v0.6.0 17 August 2026

Gem Mutations

New

  • Rolled gems now have a small chance to receive a permanent mutation.
  • Added Polished, Gilded, Prismatic, Celestial, and Corrupted mutations.
  • Mutations increase a gem's monetary value without changing its rarity or weight.
  • Mutation names and value multipliers appear on roll results and inventory cards.
  • Added a Mutation Index that permanently records discoveries and personal records.

Balance

  • Reduced Legendary gem values by 15%.
  • Reduced Mythic gem values by 25%, with Painite set to a $6,000 base value to preserve progression.
  • Reduced Exotic and Cosmic gem values by 20%, excluding Lanky Gem.
16 August 2026

Potions & progression improvements

New

  • Added the one-roll Legendary Potion and Mythic Potion.
  • Legendary Potions require 500 lifetime rolls to use.
  • Mythic Potions require 2,500 lifetime rolls to use.
  • Potions can still be crafted and collected before reaching their use requirement.

Improvements

  • Roll results now record the full effective Luck used, including potion and event boosts.
  • The Stats page now displays timed, one-roll, and Admin Event Luck correctly.
  • Luck no longer rolls gems whose rarity denominator is lower than the active Luck value.
16 August 2026

Community & Admin tools

New

  • Added owner-controlled, limited-time Admin Events.
  • Admin Events now support both additive bonuses and multiplicative modifiers.
  • Added improved loot-box rewards, including potion rewards.

Security & fixes

  • Added protection against gaining extra rolls by running the game in multiple tabs.
  • Improved server-side validation for rewards, potion use, and Admin actions.
