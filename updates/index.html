v0.11.0 22 August 2026

Guilds Reforged

Build a Crew

  • Guilds now have proper identities, usernames in the roster, three clear roles, and safer member management.
  • Guilds begin with three member slots and can permanently expand to ten.
  • Earn Guild XP, level up, and spend Guild Points on various upgrades, member capacity, and customization.
  • Daily and weekly shared missions refresh at 00:00 UTC.

Weekly Competition

  • Guilds compete globally for six days before a one-day intermission.
  • The next objective remains classified until the competition begins, while its rewards can be previewed.
  • Qualifying members earn placement rewards and guilds preserve their competition history.
  • This is a beta system; targets, rewards, and progression rates may change after live data is reviewed.
v0.10.0 21 August 2026

Season Zero — First Light

A New Track Appears

  • Season Zero introduces a 50-tier progression track lasting 30 days.
  • Genuine gem rolls award Season XP based on base rarity, with a small bonus for mutations.
  • Two daily and three weekly mission chains provide additional XP and potion rewards.
  • Each mission contains increasingly difficult milestones rather than a long list of separate tasks.

Free & Premium Rewards

  • All players can progress through and claim the Free reward track.
  • The $5,000,000 Premium Pass adds a second reward at every tier and applies retroactively.
  • Rewards include money, Potions, Enchant Relics, Ancient Relics, and permanent finale cosmetics.
  • A seven-day claim period begins after Season Zero ends.
v0.9.1.2 20 August 2026

Rare Roll & Auto Keep Hotfix

Rare Roll Chat

  • Relics and invalid catalogue results no longer appear as “Impossible” rare-roll announcements.
  • Base gems at or above 1 in 100,000 continue to receive global announcements.
  • Mutated gems now receive global announcements when their combined effective rarity reaches 1 in 1,000,000.

Auto Keep

  • Auto Keep now accepts an exact effective-rarity threshold instead of a base-rarity tier.
  • Mutation odds are included when deciding whether a roll should be protected.
  • Protection now works consistently while rolling from other pages and when automatically freeing inventory space.
v0.9.1 19 August 2026

Expedition Rebalance

Lower Entry Prices

  • Daily Standard, Deep, and Void Expeditions now cost $100,000, $500,000, and $2,000,000.
  • Weekly Standard, Deep, and Void Expeditions now cost $750,000, $3,000,000, and $10,000,000.
  • Expedition reroll prices continue to scale from the expedition's entry price.

Choose Your Reward

  • Players now select a guaranteed reward package before entering an Expedition.
  • Reward choices include focused potion bundles, Legendary and Mythic Potions, Enchant Relics, and Ancient Relics depending on difficulty.
  • Every completed Expedition still grants an additional random bonus reward.
  • The selected guaranteed reward is shown again before the entry fee is paid.

v0.8.3 Beta 19 August 2026

Masterwork Forge

Beta Feature

  • T10+ equipment can now be improved through five guaranteed Masterwork levels.
  • Masterworking consumes money, Enchant Relics, and Ancient Relics with costs scaled by equipment tier.
  • Masterwork III grants a category-specific secondary passive; Masterwork IV improves it.
  • Reforge passives randomly, use Ancient Insight to choose between three results, or Ancient Imprint to select directly.
  • Masterwork IV pickaxes can receive an enchant attunement, while Masterwork V equipment becomes Perfected.
  • Convert 12 Enchant Relics and $2,000,000 into one Ancient Relic.

Beta Notice

Costs, passive strengths, and progression may change as economy data is collected.

Late-Game Equipment

New Equipment

✨ Enchants

Enchants

  • Enchants are boosts that modify your pickaxe in different ways.
  • Each pickaxe can have only one enchant at a time.
  • Eight new enchants have been added.
  • Enchanting a pickaxe again replaces its current enchant with a guaranteed different one.

  • Relic drop chances are completely unaffected by Luck.

v0.7.1 17 August 2026

Gems Found Leaderboard

New

  • Added a Gems Found section to the Leaderboards page.
  • Gems Found is calculated by adding together the base rarity denominators of every unmutated gem rolled.
  • For example, finding gems with rarities of 1 in 2, 1 in 8, and 1 in 100 awards a total score of 110.
  • Mutated gems are excluded from the score.
  • Gems Found scores begin tracking with the release of v0.7.1.

Fixes

  • Fixed an exploit that allowed one account to roll simultaneously across multiple browser tabs.
  • Roll cooldowns are now claimed atomically by the server, so only one concurrent roll can succeed.

Balancing

  • Reworked Weight Luck with diminishing returns, preventing high bonuses from guaranteeing a gem above 1.1× weight.
  • The high-weight region now begins at a 25% chance and gradually approaches a maximum of 85%.
  • Reduced the continuation chance for each additional multiplier in the 2×+ weight tail from 1 in 2 to 1 in 3.
