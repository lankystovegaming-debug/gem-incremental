How auto-contribution works

Enable it separately on the requirements you want. A future roll contributes only when exactly one enabled, unfinished requirement matches. Bundle contributions take priority over Auto Craft. Ambiguous matches and Crown Jewel candidates stay in inventory, protected from Auto Craft and Auto Sell. Other rolls continue through your normal rules.

Locked and Museum specimens cannot be contributed. Weight requirements use final weight divided by base weight. Master unlocks after completing the first six Collections.
