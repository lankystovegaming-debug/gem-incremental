How Overall Rarity works

Overall Rarity considers your gem, successful mutations, and exceptionally heavy weights. Ordinary weights and no mutation do not increase it. It measures impressiveness, not the odds of an exact specimen.

Gem chances use square-root rarity compression. Weight uses the original weight distribution at 1× stats. Mutations after the first success have 35% of their compressed chance. Time and event requirements still apply.
