Field guide

Normal Volcanic Depths V1

Every funded depth advances through genuine gem rolls. Completing it adds Volcanic Activity before the eruption and ordinary incident checks. Rewards only arrive after the depth survives both.

Eruption Cycle

The true Eruption Point is hidden. Stable, Heating, Unstable and Critical states multiply artifact chances by ×1, ×1.15, ×1.35 and ×1.75.

Monitoring & Cooling

D3/D6/D9 unlock progressively narrower forecasts and 20%/30%/40% Cooling. A charge is consumed automatically; only one eruption can be suppressed per run.

Evacuation Lifts

Free D3/D6/D9 lifts secure up to $150K/$500K/$1.5M of economic cargo. Unused capacity expires when you descend.

Magma Sampling

Once per depth while Unstable or Critical. Sampling costs $1.5M/$3M, adds +5/+8 Activity, and succeeds 80%/65% after any eruption resolves.

Magma Chamber

Clear D10 for its cargo and independent artifact checks, then extract or enter the eruption. Overdepth is free, but OD1 is mandatory once entered.

Protected discoveries

First-copy Museum artifacts register immediately. Duplicate value remains unsecured expedition cargo until extraction and can be destroyed.
