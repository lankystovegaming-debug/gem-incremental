Field guide

How expeditions work

Expeditions run alongside normal rolling. Dispatch a crew, keep rolling gems, and decide when the haul is worth bringing home.

  1. 1

    Fund a depth

    Pay the listed cost to send the crew onward. Funding is permanent, even if the expedition ends early.

  2. 2

    Make genuine rolls

    Every genuine gem roll advances the current depth. Rare, mutated, and heavy finds can contribute extra progress.

  3. 3

    Manage the descent

    Supply Camps at D3, D6, and D9 can secure cargo or reduce Danger. Route choices after D4 and D7 trade safety for stronger cargo.

  4. 4

    Extract and settle

    Extract voluntarily to keep all cargo, or risk losing unsecured finds during a forced extraction. Museum artifacts are protected and always return.

Danger can trigger an incident when you fund the next stage, potentially destroying unsecured cargo or forcing extraction.

After D10 you can extract safely or buy an Overdepth stage for escalating rewards, cost, and Danger.
