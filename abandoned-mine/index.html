Abandoned Mine — full mechanics

Everything the crew console does not spell out: the exact costs, the Danger you face, how incidents resolve, the route forks, and the artifacts you are digging for.

The depth ladder (D1–D10)

How each roll counts

A plain roll is worth 1 progress. Notable finds stack bonuses on top of that single roll:

  • +1 rarity 1 in 50 or rarer
  • +3 rarity 1 in 1,000 or rarer
  • +7 rarity 1 in 10,000 or rarer
  • +3 any mutated gem
  • +3 displayed weight at least 2× its base

Bonuses stack: one mutated 1-in-10,000 heavyweight is worth up to 18 progress on a single roll. Only real rolls count — automation still has to actually roll.

Danger & incidents

Danger is checked only when you fund the next depth (and when you enter Overdepth). The console shows the exact percentage before you commit. If the check fires, severity is rolled:

Minor — a slice of unsecured value is lost Major — a bigger value loss, and Danger climbs further Critical — forced extraction; every unsecured find is destroyed

Museum artifacts are protected discoveries — they always return, even from a Critical. Only loose cargo is ever at risk.

Cargo, extraction & Overdepth

  • Secured cargo is banked and survives a forced extraction. Unsecured cargo is everything not yet protected by a camp or route — it is what a Major or Critical incident eats.
  • Extract voluntarily at any pause to bank all cargo and end the run cleanly.
  • Clearing D10 lets you extract safely or push into Overdepth. Each Overdepth stage is a free descent that trades your safe exit for escalating rewards and Danger, with its own artifact pool.

Hell Mode

A separate, harder descent chosen at the console. There are no Supply Camps — money only buys information, never safety. Each depth deals a hand of face-down cards; revealing one costs money, and every card you take applies a curse or lesser consequence and raises Danger and Doom. Hit the Doom threshold and a Doom Break punishes the run. Overdepth is free, and clearing D10 awards a Hell Cache — plus a guaranteed weekly Mythic Potion for up to five clears each week.
